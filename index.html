The Opportunity

The last of the raw land, in the best address in the Caribbean

Cap Cana is a ~30,000-acre gated resort community on the eastern tip of the Dominican Republic — home to Jack Nicklaus Signature golf, a world-class marina, and the region's finest beaches. After two decades of development, more than 90% of the community's property has already sold, and undeveloped land inside the gates has become genuinely scarce.

Las Palmas 104 is one of the few remaining prime parcels directly on the Punta Espada golf course. Punta Espada land has appreciated 15% or more per year over the last five years, and with raw supply now nearly exhausted, the trajectory points in one direction.
