The Investment Case

Scarce raw land, on the best course in the Caribbean, in a market that has doubled in a decade.

The Thesis

Scarcity meets a rising tide

Two forces sit behind Las Palmas 104. First, supply: raw, buildable land inside Cap Cana is nearly exhausted after two decades of development, with 90%+ of community property already sold. Land directly on the Punta Espada course — with a permanently protected view — is close to unrepeatable. Second, demand: Dominican tourism has roughly doubled in ten years, and Cap Cana has been among the Caribbean's strongest real-estate performers.

Punta Espada land specifically has appreciated 15% or more per year over the last five years. With raw supply now extremely limited and demand still climbing, the fundamentals point to continued upward pressure on prices.

Comparables & Context

Where the value sits

Completed luxury villas in Cap Cana trade broadly in the $1.6M–$5M+ range, with golf-view and new-construction homes commanding premiums. Buying the land and building lets you capture that finished-product premium yourself — while securing a view position that cannot be replicated next door.

The Dominican Republic's CONFOTUR tourism-incentive law has historically offered qualifying buyers property-transfer and property-tax relief — worth reviewing with counsel as part of your acquisition.
