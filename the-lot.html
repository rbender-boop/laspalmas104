The Buildable Envelope

Designed to capture the view

The parcel supports more than 1,500 m² of construction — comfortable room for a six- to ten-bedroom estate with generous living, wellness and guest wings.

The design intent lifts the pool deck to the second level at 9 metres, so the water line meets the horizon and the full sweep of golf course and coastline opens up. The total structure is held within Cap Cana's 12 m roof-peak height limit, keeping the estate in harmony with the community while maximising the vantage.

🔒

Your 270° view is protected — permanently

The parcel sitting between Lot 104 and the access road is owned by Cap Cana and is designated non-buildable. No future neighbour can rise to block your outlook. In a community where prime sightlines are increasingly walled in by new construction, an guaranteed-open 270° panorama is exceptionally rare.
